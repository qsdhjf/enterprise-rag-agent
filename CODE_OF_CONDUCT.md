# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome questions from newcomers,
give actionable technical feedback, and criticize ideas rather than people.
Use inclusive language across English and Chinese discussions.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language, doxxing, spam,
and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable. Do not
pressure contributors to share private enterprise data or credentials.

Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate content, warn participants or restrict
participation. Enforcement should be proportional and explain the reason while
protecting reporters' privacy. Report concerns privately to the repository owner
through the published community contact once established. A named, monitored
contact is a release-readiness requirement, not an assumed service today.
